Reading the numbers

What each number on this page tells you, and what to do with it.

Spend

What Meta spent showing the ad. Meta moves money toward ads that sell, so high spend means the ad is working.

Change vs before

This window against the one just before it. Green means your ads are picking up. Red means they're slowing and it may be time for fresh angles.

Videos vs statics

Where the money is going by format. If one side is doing all the work, that's a hint for what to brief next.

What's working

The top ads are the best brief you'll get. Watch them, note the hook and the angle, and build the next round off them.

Hook rate

Share of people who watched at least 3 seconds of a video. This is the opening: the first shot, the first line, the first caption.

Hold rate

Share of people who watched a video to the end, or 15 seconds on longer ones. This is the story and pacing after the hook.

CTR

Share of people who clicked through to the site, videos and statics. This is whether the ad made them want the product.

The columns

Every number is for the time window you pick at the top, so the column titles and numbers change when you switch windows.

Spend adds up by ad name, so moving an ad from testing to scaling keeps its total, and the same ad in every market and ad set adds up as one ad. AUD spend converts to USD at each day's ECB rate. Numbers move every hour as Meta updates.
