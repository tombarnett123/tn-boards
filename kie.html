Reading the numbers

What each number on this page means.

Credits

What Kie charged for the job, read straight from Kie once the job finishes. Different models and lengths cost different amounts.

USD

Credits times $0.005, Kie's listed price per credit. Top-up bonuses aren't counted, so this is the most it cost.

Unknown key

A job run with a key that isn't in the Keys tab of the usage sheet. Add the key ID and the editor's name there and it moves to them.

Months and days are in Sydney time. Keys expire 10 February 2027.
