How it is measured
Deadlines
Three editor deadlines: the first draft (48 hours from the brief), each round of revisions (24 hours), and the video approved within 7 days of the brief. Each one is on time or late, and late ones show by how many hours.
Hitting deadlines
At least 80% of the week's deadlines on time. Under that is Falling behind. This week shows On track so far until it drops under 80%.
Which week
A deadline counts in the week it was due. One still not done counts as late straight away and keeps growing until it is done. This week also includes every late deadline from earlier weeks that is still not done, so it shows everything the editor still owes.
Videos approved
Videos the editor got to Approved that week. Target 7. Statics are not counted. Lance (mostly the Raptor batch in Pod C) has no target yet. Jen (statics) is not on this scorecard.
Where the times come from
ClickUp resets a task's due date each time it changes stage. An hourly check records when each task moves on. First drafts and revisions before tracking began cannot be timed.
