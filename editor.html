Reading the numbers

What each number on this page tells you, and what to do with it.

Spend

What Meta spent showing the video. Meta moves money toward videos that sell, so high spend means it's working. The Spend column shows spend in the time window you pick at the top (1D, 7D, 14D and so on), so the column title and the numbers change when you switch windows: 7D spend is the last 7 days, All-time spend is everything.

Hook rate

Share of people who watched at least 3 seconds. This is your opening: the first shot, the first line, the first caption.

Hold rate

Share of people who watched to the end, or 15 seconds on longer videos. This is your pacing and story after the hook.

CTR

Share of people who clicked through to the site. This is whether the video made them want the product.

Spend adds up by ad name, so moving an ad from testing to scaling keeps its total. The same video in every market and ad set counts as one video: its spend adds up and it earns one bonus. A new version, format or UGC creator is a new video. Watch opens Meta's ad preview in a new tab. Hook rate is 3-second views over impressions, hold rate is ThruPlays over impressions, CTR is link clicks over impressions.
